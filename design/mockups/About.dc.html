About Open Chiari

Open Chiari is a free, independent website that helps people see and understand Chiari malformation, and points them to the organizations and research moving the field forward.

How the content is made

  • Medical facts are adapted from the NINDS fact sheet and peer-reviewed research, listed on each page.
  • The 3D model is built from Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D, open anatomy data.
  • Text and every 3D view are checked by [reviewer name, credentials] before they're published. Last review: [date].
  • AI tools helped write the code and first drafts; every page is checked by a person.
