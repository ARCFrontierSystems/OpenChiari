This is what my doctor meant by Chiari. The low part of my brain is crowding the opening at the base of my skull, which is why coughing gives me headaches.

What you're looking at

  1. The dashed line runs across the opening where the brain meets the spinal cord.
  2. The lowest part of the cerebellum, the tonsils, sits below that line. The blue bracket shows how far.
  3. That crowds the space where spinal fluid normally flows, which can cause headaches and other symptoms.

How you can help

Symptoms can come and go and are often invisible. Believing them, and making room for rest, helps more than you might think.
