# Independent audit: Simple 3D model and figures (4 conditions)

- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Who:** a fresh AI agent that did not build the model (CLAUDE.md rule 1a, check 3). Not a medical review.
- **Scope:** the four reference figures (`design/figures/`), the 3D Explorer at the midline in all four conditions, the shape data (`src/lib/anatomy-shapes.json`) and the automatic measurements, against the "3D model shapes", "Syrinx" and "After decompression" rows of EVIDENCE.md.
- **Limitation:** every cited source page was blocked by this environment's network settings (PubMed Central, Radiopaedia, AJNR, QxMD, Manipal). Quotes come from search-engine abstract snippets, so this is a second-hand source check.
- **Status:** findings 1 to 4 change the approved figure and model shapes, and need Matthew's decision (CLAUDE.md: ask before changing any figure). The MRI comparison (`docs/audits/`) is meant to settle the proportions in 3 and 4.

| 1 | High | **Fluid pathway not continuous.** In Typical, the spinal fluid starts flat at the McRae line, the cisterna magna is a separate sliver, and there is no fluid around the tonsils at the foramen magnum; After decompression, the re-formed space is not joined to the fluid behind the cord. ## Checked and OK

Foramen magnum 35.3 mm (source not opened); typical tonsil 0.9 mm above and rounded (meta-analysis pooled mean +1.13 mm); Chiari I tonsil 12.4 mm below and pointed, at the C1 arch; no cisterna in Chiari I; brainstem and fourth ventricle unchanged; dens tip below the basion and in front of the cord; syrinx central, beaded, 5 mm in an 11 mm cord; C1 arch removed; dural patch; captions present, midline note in the tonsil card.
