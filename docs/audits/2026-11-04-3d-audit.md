# Independent audit: Simple 3D model and figures (4 conditions)

- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Who:** a fresh AI agent that did not build the model (CLAUDE.md rule 1a, check 3). Not a medical review.
- **Scope:** the four reference figures (`design/figures/`), the 3D Explorer at the midline in all four conditions, the shape data (`src/lib/anatomy-shapes.json`) and the automatic measurements, against the "3D model shapes", "Syrinx" and "After decompression" rows of EVIDENCE.md.
- **Limitation:** every cited source page was blocked by this environment's network settings (PubMed Central, Radiopaedia, AJNR, QxMD, Manipal). Quotes come from search-engine abstract snippets, so this is a second-hand source check.
- **Status:** findings 1 to 4 change the approved figure and model shapes, and need Matthew's decision (CLAUDE.md: ask before changing any figure). The MRI comparison (`docs/audits/`) is meant to settle the proportions in 3 and 4.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Suggested fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | High | **Fluid pathway not continuous.** In Typical, the spinal fluid starts flat at the McRae line, the cisterna magna is a separate sliver, and there is no fluid around the tonsils at the foramen magnum; After decompression, the re-formed space is not joined to the fluid behind the cord. The site says fluid "moves freely around them", and the point of Chiari is that the tonsils block a normally open pathway (Milhorat 1999: "obliteration of the retrocerebellar cerebrospinal fluid spaces"). | Typical: continuous fluid from the fourth ventricle outlet, through the cisterna magna, around the tonsils, through the foramen magnum into the fluid behind the cord. Chiari I: the tonsil plugs it. After decompression: join the re-formed space to the fluid behind the cord. |
| 2 | Medium | **Posterior fossa, clivus and tentorium identical in Typical and Chiari I.** EVIDENCE.md says Chiari I has a smaller posterior fossa, a clivus a few mm shorter and a steeper tentorium; COPY says the back of the skull is "smaller or shaped differently". Note: Sekula 2005 studied patients *without* tonsillar descent below 3 to 5 mm, so it doesn't fully support the tentorium claim for Chiari I with descent. | Chiari I: shorten the clivus about 4 mm, steepen the tentorium, slightly shrink the occipital curve; find a Chiari I source for the tentorium or drop that claim. Or mark these rows "not shown in the Simple model". |
| 3 | Medium | **Clivus too long.** Dorsum sellae to basion is about 62 mm in the figure (about 50 mm from the top of the clivus). EVIDENCE.md: 39 to 47 mm (definition: dorsum sellae to basion on the midline image). | About 44 mm (Typical), about 40 mm (Chiari I). |

Adult MRI studies (snippets): cord about 8.8 mm at C2, 7.6 mm at C3; canal about 13.7 mm at C3. ## Checked and OK

Foramen magnum 35.3 mm (source not opened); typical tonsil 0.9 mm above and rounded (meta-analysis pooled mean +1.13 mm); Chiari I tonsil 12.4 mm below and pointed, at the C1 arch; no cisterna in Chiari I; brainstem and fourth ventricle unchanged; dens tip below the basion and in front of the cord; syrinx central, beaded, 5 mm in an 11 mm cord; C1 arch removed; dural patch; captions present, midline note in the tonsil card.
