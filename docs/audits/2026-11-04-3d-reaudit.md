## Checked and OK

- **Foramen magnum:** 35.3 mm. PMC9834072 controls "36.23 ± 3.26"; PMC6703074 "36.9±2.9" / "38.0±3.0" mm. `model:check`: 35.3.
- **Typical tonsil:** tip 1.1 mm above the line (3D: 0.9), rounded (9.4 mm wide 3 mm above the tip; 3D: 10.2).
- **Chiari I tonsil:** tip 12.6 mm below (3D: 12.4), pointed (5.1 mm wide; 3D: 6.8), level with the C1 back arch. PMC9834072: "12.4 ± 3.7 (5.0-25.4)".
- **After surgery:** tip 10.6 mm below (3D: 10.5), a rise of 2.0 mm (3D: 1.9), still below the line.
- **Bone removed:** 24.4 mm along the skull from the opisthion (3D: 28 x 30 mm); C1 back arch middle 25 mm removed. PMC11912941: "the 3 cm × 2.5 cm craniectomy"; "Approximately 2.5 cm of the middle part of the posterior arch of the atlas was removed"; PMC10478704: "3 cm in diameter around the foramen magnum". Dural patch present; PMC11912941: "The dura mater was sutured with an artificial dural patch."
- **Spinal cord:** 9.0 mm front to back from C2 to C5 (3D: 9.2 at C2); 11 mm at the foramen magnum. PMC8334714: "8.8 mm x 12.4 mm at C2". PMC6703074 gives 7.7 to 7.8 mm, so the model sits at the high end. Cord 12 mm side to side in 3D.
- **Neck canal at C3:** 14.3 mm (3D: 14.8), closed at the back by the laminae; fluid about 2.3 mm in front of the cord and 3 mm behind. PMC2899662: "13.73 ± 1.37 mm". Canal-to-body ratio about 1.0 against the Pavlov ratio "0.94±0.1" / "0.88±0.1".
- **Typical fluid path:** one continuous piece (`model:check`) from the fourth ventricle outlet down to the neck: at 4 mm above the line, 8 mm of fluid in front of the tonsil and 13 mm behind it; 4 to 6 mm behind the cerebellum.
- **Chiari I fluid path:** blocked behind the tonsil (fluid starts 1.2 mm below the line); fluid behind the cord at the opening is 4 mm, which fits the "3.5 times" pre-surgery size in PMC10549945.
- **After-surgery fluid path:** the re-formed space is joined to the fluid behind the cord (one piece, up to 30 mm above the line).
- **Syrinx:** central in the cord (off-center by 0.9 mm at most), beaded, 2.9 to 4.6 mm in a 9 mm cord (32 to 51%), starting mid-C3, 49 mm below the line, and running off the bottom of the view. NINDS: syrinx "usually in the spine's cervical (neck) region". PMC9834072 split patients into syrinx-to-cord ratios below and above 50%.
