# Independent audit: realistic 3D model (4 conditions)

- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Who:** a fresh AI agent that did not build the model (CLAUDE.md rule 1a, check 3). This is not a medical review.
- **Scope:** the realistic model (Z-Anatomy based) in Typical, Chiari I, Chiari I with syrinx, and After decompression: the raw builds (`/tmp/claude-0/za/view/*-raw.glb`), the Explorer files (`model/realistic/web/*.glb`), the build script (`model/realistic/extract.py`, README), the automatic checks (`model/realistic/check.mjs`), and the visible text (`src/i18n/en.json`: explorer.conditions, explorer.parts, figure). Compared with `docs/EVIDENCE.md` ("3D model shapes", "Syrinx", "After decompression").
- **Sources opened (text read):** PMC9834072 (Polish J Radiol, posterior fossa morphometry); PMC8334714 (cord size, abstract); PMC2899662 (neck canal); PMC10549945 (changes after decompression); PMC11912941 and PMC10478704 (size of bone removed).
- **How:** ran all four automatic checks (all pass), then measured the geometry myself with small scripts: midline and off-midline sections, cross-sections of the neck, a full inside/outside test for every structure pair that matters, volumes, and the web files. Rendered cut views of Typical, Chiari I and After decompression to look at them. Scripts were deleted afterwards.
- **Limitations:** no MRI comparison here (see the separate MRI report). Some atlas surfaces are not fully closed, so inside/outside counts on atlas-only parts (cerebellum, tentorium) are approximate. I did not rebuild the model from the atlas. Judging what a patient will understand from a picture is my opinion, not a measurement.

## Checked and OK

- **Foramen magnum (McRae line):** 38.4 mm, basion (−19.0, 2.5) to opisthion (19.0, −2.8). PMC9834072: controls "36.23 ± 3.26 (30.2-46.5)"; Chiari I 35.88 ± 4.58, "p = 0.689" (no difference), so using the same opening in all views is right.
- **Typical tonsils:** tip 0.9 mm above the line (EVIDENCE: about 1 mm above). Rounded: 13.7 mm front to back 3 mm above the tip.
- **Chiari I tonsils:** tip 12.4 mm below the line (PMC9834072: "12.4 ± 3.7 (5.0-25.4)"). Peg-shaped: 6.9 × 5.0 mm at 3 mm above the tip and 3.7 × 2.7 mm at 1 mm above. The tip reaches the level of the top of the C1 back arch (12.8 mm below the line), matching EVIDENCE "often reaches the C1 back arch" (PMC9834072 grade 2: "the tonsils had reached the C1 arch").
- **No tonsil passes into anything:** a full inside test found 0 tonsil points inside the occipital bone, C1, C2, skull, cord, brainstem or dural patch, in all four views.
- **After decompression, tonsils:** tip 10.4 mm below the line, risen 2.0 mm. PMC10549945: "decreased 2 mm 3–6 months after surgery". Still below the line, as EVIDENCE says.
- **Bone removed:** a window 30 mm across, running about 25 mm up the bone from the opisthion; bone resumes about 25 mm back at the midline. PMC11912941: "a 2.5× 3 cm bone window"; PMC10478704: "3 cm in diameter around the foramen magnum". **C1:** a gap of 25.0 mm in the middle of the back arch (PMC11912941: "Approximately 2.5 cm of the middle part of the posterior arch of the atlas was removed").
- **Re-formed fluid:** behind the tonsils at the opening; reaches 19.6 mm below the line and meets the top of the spinal dura (about 1 mm apart). Fluid volume around the cerebellum: Typical 95 ml, Chiari I 84 ml, After decompression 100 ml. The patch touches neither the cerebellum nor the tonsils.
- **Spinal cord:** 8.8 mm front to back and 12.4 mm side to side at C2; 8.7 × 13.0 mm near C4 (PMC8334714: "8.8 mm x 12.4 mm at C2 to 8.7 mm x 14 mm at C4").
- **Syrinx:** fully inside the cord (0 of 4,168 points outside) and centred in it. It measures 41 to 49% of the cord front to back and about 38% side to side, and is beaded. It runs from 46 mm below the line (C2–C3) to the bottom of the view (98 mm, about C5), so it is in the neck part. It looks about 2.5 vertebrae long only because the view ends there (EVIDENCE: about 5 on average; can start as high as C1).
- **Clivus (Typical):** 43.6 mm from dorsum sellae to basion (PMC9834072 controls "38.99 ± 2.67 (31.6-46.2)"; EVIDENCE about 39 to 47 mm).
- **Fourth ventricle:** the lowest point is 6.6 mm above the line and does not move in Chiari I (normal position, as EVIDENCE says). The fastigium is 31.0 mm above the line (controls 27.43 ± 2.98, range 20.2 to 35.4).
- **Dens:** tip 10.0 mm below the line, directly below the basion (10.1 mm from it), 7.8 mm in front of the cord.
- **Typical cisterna magna:** open, about 10 mm deep behind the lower vermis at the midline.
- **Explorer files:** desktop matches the raw builds (opening 38.4 mm; tips +0.9, −12.4, −10.4 mm). Phone is within 0.3 mm (38.3 mm; +1.2, −12.1, −10.2 mm). In both, the tonsils stay clear of bone and cord, the syrinx stays inside the cord, and each part is tagged with the right views.
- **Visible text:** the condition notes, part cards and screen-reader descriptions match what the model shows (12 mm, pointed tonsils, bone and C1 arch removed, patch). Exceptions are findings 1 and 7.
