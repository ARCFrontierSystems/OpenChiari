# Independent audit: this week's text (tour, side by side, Awareness kit, Explain It, Research studies) and a sweep of en.json

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Who:** a fresh AI agent that did not write any of this text (CLAUDE.md rule 1a, check 3). This is a check against sources, not a medical review.
- **Scope:** `src/i18n/en.json` keys `tour`, `compare`, `kit`, `explain`, `studies`; what `scripts/make-kit.mjs` puts on the social post, quote cards, wallet cards, poster and ZIP (the built files in `public/kit/` were opened and looked at); the pages that show this text (`src/pages/tour.astro`, `explorer/compare.astro`, `explain.astro`/`v.astro`, `kit.astro`, `studies.astro`). Then a sweep of every other medical sentence in `en.json`, focused on the sections that no earlier text audit covered (support, about, privacy, model, impact, embed). Home, Calm, Explorer, prep and figure text were fully audited on Nov 4 (`2026-11-04-text-reaudit.md`) and have not changed since except for the approved fixes, so they were only re-read.
- **Result:** about 60 sentences and captions checked. **15 problems: 0 High, 5 Medium, 10 Low.** Every medical sentence in `en.json` matches `docs/COPY.md` word for word (or a change recorded there as approved). No sentence reads as medical advice, and no page claims a medical professional reviewed it.
- **Sources opened for this audit:** Baisden 2012, PMC3422094 (the saved copy in `docs/audits/sources/2026-11-05-symptoms/`, read in full for the crowding and fluid-flow sentences); MedlinePlus "Chiari Malformation" health topic (https://medlineplus.gov/chiarimalformation.html, opened 2026-11-05).
- **Could not open:** NINDS and Cleveland Clinic (blocked by this environment's network settings); PubMed Central (PMC11371388, PMC12888059, PMC5615451, PMC9834072) shows a robot check, which was not bypassed; michigan.gov (not attempted after the robot-check refusal). Where a finding relies on these, it uses the quotes recorded by the Nov 4 audits, which did open them, and says so.

## Summary in plain language

The new pages mostly reuse sentences that were already checked, and the wording matches the approved copy. The problems are mostly about **what's missing around the pictures**, plus **two sentences that state as always true something the sources say happens often**:

1. **The note that the tonsils are drawn "at their lowest point, where MRI measures them" is missing** from the guided tour, side by side, the Explain It pages, and the Awareness kit's social post and poster. All of these show Chiari I. CLAUDE.md says this note must go with the figure. The approved sentence already exists, so the fix is to add it, not to write new text.
2. **The Explain It "make a link" page shows the model with no "Illustration, not a medical image" or "Fluid flow simplified" caption.**
3. **The tour and side by side say the tonsils "fill the space where fluid should pass"**, and Explain It says Chiari "crowds the space where spinal fluid normally flows". The sources and the rest of the site say this happens **often, not always**. The Explain It sentence also has no row in EVIDENCE.md, although the Baisden article (already a source) supports it.
4. **About and Impact say every text and 3D view is independently checked "before they're published"**, with "Last check: November 4, 2026 · passed". The tour and side by side were published on November 5 before this check. Those lines need a new date once Matthew approves the fixes, and new pages should wait for the check.
5. Smaller points: three pages and the poster leave out "Talk to your doctor about your own care"; the kit uses a slightly different model credit from the exact one in CLAUDE.md; the social post's headline says "explained" but the picture has no labels or words; and a few record-keeping and wording items.

Nothing here is dangerous. All fixes that touch medical text need Matthew's approval (CLAUDE.md rule 1).

## Checked and fine

**Guided tour (`tour`)**
- Every step's text matches COPY.md "Guided tour, all five steps" word for word, including the two approved rephrasings. Step titles, "Finish tour", "Tour progress", "Next: {title}" are recorded as approved.
- Step 1: "This view is cut down the middle…" is true. The tour starts from the Explorer's starting state, which is the midline cut (`cut: 50`), and the 2D fallback is a midline section. "The cerebellum helps control balance and coordination" (row 1, Cleveland cerebellum). The foramen magnum sentence matches the structure card (Cleveland medulla, opened Nov 4).
- Step 2: matches the approved Typical note (PMC11371388 "lowest normal tonsillar position was considered 3 mm", opened Nov 4).
- Step 3: the mockup text. It is the definition; the view shows Chiari I with the dashed line and bracket (`measure: true`).
- Step 4: the CSF sentences are row 9 (PMC5615451, "a fast movement synchronized with the heartbeat", opened Nov 4; "Fluid flow simplified" caption shown). "Red marks show where flow is often restricted" is hedged and the red marks are drawn in that step (`flow: true` on Chiari I). Only the "fill the space" part is flagged (item 3).
- Step 5: the approved syrinx definition (NINDS syringomyelia, opened Nov 4).
- Footer: "Illustration, not a medical image · Fluid flow simplified", the evidence link (follows the condition), "Not medical advice", NINDS credit and the exact model credit. The screen-reader label follows each step's condition.
