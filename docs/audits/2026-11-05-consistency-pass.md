# Design and consistency pass

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Who:** a fresh AI agent that did not build the site. This is a look-and-feel polish check, not a medical review. Nothing in the site was changed.
- **What was tested:** the **live site** (openchiari.org) in Chromium through Playwright. 15 pages (`/`, `/explorer/`, `/explorer/compare/`, `/tour/`, `/explain/`, `/prep/`, `/children/`, `/studies/`, `/kit/`, `/model/`, `/support/`, `/about/`, `/privacy/`, `/impact/`, `/evidence/`) in English and Spanish, at desktop (1280 x 900) and phone (390 x 844): 60 page views, with viewport and full-page screenshots, plus measurements of headings, intros, buttons and overflow. Dim mode was tried on 11 pages (desktop) and 4 (phone), Calm mode on 5 (phone) and 4 (desktop), and Dim plus Calm on 2. The 3D used software graphics.
- **Rules checked against:** `docs/DESIGN.md` (type sizes, one accent, purple for brand only, 4 px corners, 44 px touch targets, hairline rules, "2 px ink rule above major lists and tables").
- **Screenshots** are in the session scratchpad, not in the repository: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-OpenChiari/913aa686-fc2f-5b5e-a5e1-c086ad3c1edb/scratchpad/consistency/`. Names are `<page>_<en|es>_<d|m>_<top|full>.png`; phone pages are also cut into side-by-side strips as `<page>_<lang>_m_sheet<N>.png`. Dim and Calm shots start with `dim_`, `calm_` or `dimcalm_`.

## Summary in plain language

The site looks like one product. The colors, fonts, buttons and footer are the same everywhere, nothing scrolls sideways on phones, Dim mode adapts almost everything, and the Spanish pages are fully translated apart from a few deliberate exceptions. The problems are about **things that are cut off**, **actions that are hard to find on a phone**, and **the same thing having different names or sizes on different pages**.

What matters most:

1. **On phones, the 3D Explorer cuts off its own content.** The panel under the model is a small box that scrolls by itself inside the page. "What happens in surgery, step by step" is half hidden behind the footer in English and **completely hidden in Spanish**. With Calm mode on, the cough question is cut off too.
2. **On the Spanish Explain It page (desktop), the "Correo electrónico" button text spills out of its button**, and "Mensaje de texto" touches both edges.
3. **On phones, Explain It opens with only the preview picture.** The page title, the note and the "Copy note and link" button are one to two screens further down.
4. **The Impact quality table is broken on phones, especially in Spanish.** The last column ("What it means") is squeezed to one letter wide and cut off, so each row becomes very tall and unreadable.
5. **The Awareness kit's "For organizations" box says to use "Make your own" *below*, but it sits *after* that section.** Its button scrolls you back up.
6. **Page titles come in three sizes.** Home, Studies, Kit, Impact and Evidence use the large size (54 px); Prep, Children, Model, Support, About and Privacy use 42 px; Explain It uses 40 px. Section headings come in five sizes (26 to 36 px) and four different rule styles.
7. **The visit-prep tool has four names:** "Visit prep" (menu), "Doctor visit prep" (Home), "Prepare for your next appointment" (page title) and "Visit prep questions" (Privacy). Spanish has three. The Support page has a similar problem, and Spanish shows "Explain It" in some places and "Explain It (Explíquelo)" in others.
8. **On phones, Research studies hides "What you'd do", the ClinicalTrials.gov link and the patient-registry note.** The study title is the only link, and it doesn't look like a link.
9. **The three dark 3D pages have three different headers.** The Explorer has Home, the logo and a Calm switch. Side by side has no Home button and no Calm switch, and on phones no logo. The tour has no logo, no Home and no Calm switch.
10. **"Not medical advice" appears twice on many pages, worded differently each time.** It is in the footer and again in a page note (Prep, Children, Model, Studies).

**Calm mode works**: bigger text, softer colors, "Calm on" label, the hero swaps the tour button for a note, and nothing overflows. Dim and Calm together look right on Home and Prep (`dimcalm_home_en_d_full.png`, `dimcalm_prep_en_d_full.png`).

## Spanish parity

- Every Spanish page shows the translation notice and is translated, apart from deliberate exceptions: study titles and sponsors (official names), organization names, the citation and embed code, the pronunciation box (L5), and the Evidence notes body, which the page says is in English.
- Layout problems from longer Spanish text: H1 (Explorer sheet hides a whole section), H2 (Explain buttons), H4 (Impact table), L2 (Prep button wraps), L7 (nav crowding).
- English left on Spanish pages: the browser's "Choose File / No file chosen" on the Kit (M21), and "Explain It" without a translation in places (M8).
- The translation banner pushes every Spanish page down by 45 px on desktop and about 100 px on phones. That is expected and fine.

## What was not checked

Medical wording (not in scope), keyboard focus and screen readers, screens wider than 1280 px or between 390 and 1280 px, print output, the `/mri/`, `/embed/` and `/v/` pages, and real phones.
