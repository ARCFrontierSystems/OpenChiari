# Site check-up 2: today's new features

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Who:** a fresh AI agent that did not build the site. This checks how the site looks and works. It is not a medical review.
- **What was tested:** the built site in `dist/` (built 07:30 UTC), served locally with `astro preview`, in Chromium through Playwright. There are 32 pages: 16 English pages and their Spanish twins. Each page was tested at desktop (1440 x 900) and on Playwright's "iPhone 13" (390 x 664, touch), in light colors and in Dim, with full-page screenshots, console errors, failed requests, sideways scroll, internal links and an axe scan. Calm and Dim together were tested on 13 pages. The 3D used software graphics, so speed was not judged.
- **Live site:** openchiari.org uses the same file names as this build, and three pages compared pixel by pixel were identical. Two problems (items 1 and 2) exist **only on the live site**: Cloudflare adds them, and they are not in the code.
- **Note:** at 08:23 someone else rebuilt `dist/`, which now includes source changes that are not yet committed. All the tests below ran before that, against the 07:30 build that matches the live site.
- Screenshots and scripts are in the session scratchpad (`/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-OpenChiari/913aa686-fc2f-5b5e-a5e1-c086ad3c1edb/scratchpad/checkup2/`), not in the repository. In the table, screenshot names are in `shots/`, `parts/` (crops) or `pairs/` (light and Dim side by side).

## Summary in plain language

The site works, and so do almost all of today's features. Every page loaded in both languages, at both sizes, in light and in Dim, with no errors. Nothing scrolled sideways on phones, and every internal link worked. The automatic accessibility scan found **no problems on any page**, in light colors, in Dim, or with Calm and Dim on together. Dim mode, Listen, the Explain It picture, "See it in your room", the plaques download, "How do you say it?", the quarters size line and the cough panel all do what they should. Calm mode stops all motion. Every Spanish page shows the translation notice, and the language link keeps you on the same page and the same 3D view.

What matters most:

1. **The live site loads Cloudflare's visitor-statistics script from another web address** (`static.cloudflareinsights.com`). It isn't in the site's code: Cloudflare adds it to every page on its way out. You chose not to use Cloudflare Web Analytics, the Privacy page doesn't mention it, and the site's rules say visitors' browsers make no requests to other companies. It is probably switched on in the Cloudflare dashboard (Web Analytics for openchiari.org). I couldn't check what it records.
2. **Cloudflare also hides the contact email on the live site.** On About and Privacy, `hello@openchiari.org` only appears after a Cloudflare script runs. Without that script it reads "[email protected]". That happens with JavaScript off, and probably offline too, because the offline helper doesn't save that script. This is Cloudflare's "Email Address Obfuscation" setting.
3. **On desktop, the Spanish header always takes two rows.** "Modo tranquilo" (Calm mode) drops onto its own line under the logo at every screen width.
4. **On dark backgrounds, keyboard focus on Listen buttons is hard to see.** The ring is dark blue on near-black (Explorer, tour).
5. **On the Spanish page, Listen reads the English pronunciation guide with a Spanish voice.** It reads "key-AR-ee" as Spanish, which defeats the point of "How do you say it?".
6. **"See it in your room" shows the model without the model credit or "Not medical advice".** It only shows the name and the caption. I couldn't test it on a real iPhone.

The rest are small items, mostly about the offline helper and polish.

## Checked and fine

- **Loading, errors, links:** all 32 pages × 2 sizes × light and Dim (128 loads) gave no page errors, no console errors (apart from the test machine's WebGL warnings) and no failed requests. The built site requests nothing from other websites. All 54 internal addresses return 200. In-page links (`#content`, `#viewer`, `#views`, `/impact/#open-source`, the Evidence sections in both languages) all exist. The footer "Open source" link now lands on its section.
- **Phone layout:** no sideways scroll on any page in either language or color mode. The Impact quality table now scrolls inside its own box.
- **Accessibility scan (axe, WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A and AA):** 0 violations on all 32 pages at both sizes, in light and Dim, and on 13 pages with Calm and Dim on together.
- **Dim mode:** turns on by itself when the system is set to dark. The header switch (desktop) and the menu switch (phone) turn it on and off, by mouse, tap or keyboard (Space). The choice is saved, wins over the system setting both ways, and carries across pages and languages. No white flash between pages (first painted frames recorded). Figures keep their labels in Dim (Home Fig. 1 checked up close). Primary buttons switch to light fill with dark text. Logos switch to the light version. The printed visit-prep sheet stays black on white with Dim on.
- **Calm and Dim together:** Dim's colors with Calm's 19 px text and larger buttons. The "Calm mode is on" note shows. Nothing unreadable seen.
- **Listen:** visible on Home (3 sections), each tour step, the Explorer card (desktop) and condition note (phone), and the Explain It recipient page, in both languages. It turns to "Stop"/"Detener" (aria-pressed true) and back. Each block is read once (no repeats), only the visible version (desktop vs phone wording), in the page's language (`en-US` / `es-US`). Starting a second Listen resets the first. It goes back to "Listen" when the reading ends.
- **Offline helper:** on the live site, Home, Explorer, tour, `/es/` and the recipient page open offline from the saved copy, with their scripts and fonts. `/api/` is never saved: no `/api/` entry after browsing and calling it, and an offline call to `/api/count` fails instead of being answered from the device. Downloads aren't saved. (On the local preview, scripts and fonts failed offline because the preview server sends `Vary: Origin`. That is a test-server quirk; the live site doesn't do it.)
- **Explain It picture:** a 1200 × 1500 PNG downloads (`open-chiari-syrinx.png`), with "The picture was saved to your downloads." It carries the view name, "Illustration, not a medical image. Fluid flow simplified. Tonsils shown at their lowest point, where MRI measures them.", "Not medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your own care.", the exact model credit and openchiari.org. The test note ("SECRETNOTE…") was **not** on it. Same in Spanish and on the phone.
- **See it in your room:** hidden without AR support. With iPhone support faked, the button appears (desktop toolbar, phone panel; "Véalo en su habitación" in Spanish) and produces a `blob:` link with `rel="ar"` to a real USDZ file (`model/vnd.usdz+zip`, a zip starting with `model.usda`).
- **3D-printable plaques:** `openchiari-print.zip` (5.6 MB) downloads and holds `openchiari-midline-typical.stl`, `openchiari-midline-chiari.stl` (valid binary STL, about 149,000 triangles each), `README.txt` (with the captions, "Not medical advice" and the credit) and `LICENSE.md`.
- **How do you say it?** It shows on Home in both languages and at both sizes, marked "(en inglés)" in Spanish.
- **Size line (quarters):** shows only for Chiari I and Chiari I with syrinx with Measure on. It is hidden for Typical and After surgery, and when Measure is off (desktop and phone).
- **Cough panel:** shows only for Typical and Chiari I. "Cough" announces "During the cough: some spinal fluid moves up…" then "Then it flows back down.", and it is disabled while playing. The measure bracket and dashed line hide during the cough and come back afterwards. In Calm mode (and with reduced motion) nothing animates: one still "pushed up" picture, then back.
- **Translation notice and language link:** the notice is on all 16 Spanish pages and none of the English ones. The language link goes to the same page in the other language on every page that has one. On `/explorer/`, `/explain/`, `/v/` and `/embed/` it keeps the view after the #.
- **Keyboard:** on Home, a skip link comes first, then the logo, nav, Español, Dim, Calm, the hero buttons, the three Listen buttons, the tools and the footer. On the Explorer: header, conditions, layers, cut-away, model, 3D view, then Fluid flow / Labels / Measure / camera (now before the labels, an improvement), labels, parts list, the cough panel, Listen and the footer. Focus is visible everywhere except item 4. Enter on Listen starts reading.
- **Printed visit-prep sheet:** now carries the model credit under the diagram.
