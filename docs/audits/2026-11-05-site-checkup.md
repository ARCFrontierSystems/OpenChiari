# Site check-up before promotion

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Who:** a fresh AI agent that did not build the site. This is a check of how the site looks and works, not a medical review.
- **What was tested:** the built site in `dist/` (built 2026-11-05 03:43 UTC), served locally with `astro preview`, in Chromium through Playwright. 17 pages, each at desktop (1440 x 900), phone (390 x 844) and Playwright's "iPhone 13" (390 x 664, touch). Software graphics, so 3D speed was not judged.
- **Important:** while this check ran, the working copy had 25 changed source files that are not yet built or committed (Spanish work, and fixes from the parallel accuracy audit `2026-11-05-checkup-accuracy-audit.md`). Some problems below may already be fixed in that unbuilt code; where I could see that, I say so. Rebuild and re-check before promoting.
- Screenshots and scripts are in the session scratchpad (`.../scratchpad/checkup/shots/`), not in the repository. Filenames are listed in the table.

## Summary in plain language

The site is in good shape. Every page loads, nothing is broken or missing, there are no errors, no outside requests, and every internal link and download works. The 3D Explorer, side by side, tour, Explain It, visit prep and Calm mode all do what they should, on desktop and on a touch phone. An automatic accessibility scan found no problems on any page at either size, and keyboard focus is visible.

Before you promote it, these matter most:

1. **The "Español" link goes to an English page that says "Not built yet."** It is in the header and footer of every page. (Spanish work is in progress in the unbuilt code.)
2. **The Impact page scrolls sideways on phones**, and its quality table is cut off at the right edge. The page also shows "Not counted yet" and "Not measured yet" in every box, and "Launched November 4, 2026", which makes the site look unfinished.
3. **Some unfinished notes are visible to the public:** the Support page says "[Before launch: confirm with each organization.]", and the Evidence notes page has to-do lists and file names like "CLAUDE.md" and "docs/audits/".
4. **On phones, choosing a part in the Explorer's parts list doesn't show its description.** The description is further down inside a small scrolling box, and nothing brings it into view.
5. **Three pages (tour, side by side, embed) leave out "Talk to your doctor about your own care."** and the Explain It page shows the model without "Illustration, not a medical image". These are rule 1 items; the parallel accuracy audit also found them, and the footer fix is already in the unbuilt code.
6. **Some 3D views look empty or broken:** at the far end of the cut-away slider the head becomes a floating sliver with labels pointing at nothing, and the Back, Front and Below camera views show only the outside of half a head. If someone shares a Back view with Explain It, the person who opens it sees no brain, even though the text describes the dashed line and blue bracket.

The rest are small polish items.

## Checked and fine

- **Loading and errors:** all 17 pages loaded at all three sizes with no page errors, no console errors and no failed requests. The only console messages were WebGL performance warnings from the test machine's software graphics. The site made **no requests to any other website** (fonts, 3D files and data are all self-hosted). `/api/count` and `/api/totals` weren't called on the local preview.
- **Links:** all 26 internal addresses linked from the pages return 200 (pages, the three model downloads, the kit files). In-page links (`#content`, `#viewer`, `#views`, `/evidence/#3d-model-shapes`) exist, except item 12. 88 external links noted, not visited.
- **Downloads:** kit ZIP (1.7 MB; 6 files plus LICENSE and CREDIT), social post PNG, both quote cards, wallet card PDF, Letter and A4 posters, and the glTF, Blender and figure-set ZIPs all return 200 with the right file type and a non-zero size.
- **Phone layout:** no sideways scrolling on any page except `/impact/` (item 2). No missing images, overlapping text or cut-off buttons apart from items 7 and 13. The phone menu opens, lists all main pages, and closes with Escape.
- **Footer and credits:** every page using the main footer has "Not medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your own care." and the exact model credit. Every page showing the model or a render has the credit, and all except `/explain/` and `/model/` Fig. 2 show "Illustration, not a medical image" (items 9, 20).
- **3D Explorer (desktop):** all four conditions switch the view and its screen-reader description. Layer on/off and opacity work. The cut-away has 5 steps with matching descriptions. The parts list selects a part and shows its card. Search works ("cord" shows Spinal cord; nonsense shows nothing). Clicking a label in the view selects that part. Fluid flow, Labels and Measure toggle on and off. The Side, Back, Below, Front and reset buttons work. Keyboard: the view takes focus; arrow keys turn it ("Turned view"), + zooms, 2 gives the back view, 0 resets. The Realistic model loads and draws. Phone: condition buttons, Flow, Measure, Realistic, the Layers panel (layers, cut-away, camera), and touch-drag rotation all work.
- **Side by side:** both menus offer all four conditions, the notes follow them, and "Rotate together" works both ways (on: dragging or arrow keys on one view turn both; off: only that one turns).
- **Guided tour:** Next goes through all five steps (Meet the model, Typical anatomy, The tonsils sit too low, How fluid flows, A syrinx). Back goes back, with no Back button on step 1. "Finish tour" and "Exit tour" go to the Explorer.
- **Explain It:** the link follows the Explorer view (`/explain/#syr.back.f.m.l.x`). The 280-character limit holds. "Let them rotate" adds or removes `.x`. "Copy note and link" puts the note, a blank line and the link on the clipboard and says "Copied your note and the link." Text message and Email open with the note and link filled in. The QR code dialog opens and closes. **The note never appeared in the link or in any network request.** The recipient page opens the right view with the right caption, and a broken or missing fragment falls back to Chiari I.
- **Visit prep:** ticking and unticking works. Your own questions can be added with the button or Enter, and empty ones are ignored. "Print sheet" and "Save as PDF" open the print dialog. The print view is a clean one-page Letter sheet with answer lines, "Things I want to mention", the diagram and its caption, and "Not medical advice". Nothing is saved: after reloading, everything is cleared and nothing is stored.
- **Calm mode:** turning it on sets 19 px text and softer colors and saves it on the device. It stays on after reloading and on other pages, including the Explorer, and turns off and stays off. It also turns on by itself when the system asks for reduced motion. The phone header switch works too.
- **Open model:** the embed code updates for all four starting views and the three options. Each generated embed opens the chosen view, with Calm mode and labels as set. "Copy embed code" copies it. An embed with Calm mode on doesn't switch Calm mode on for the main site.
- **Research studies:** two recruiting studies with ClinicalTrials.gov links, the processed date and "Summaries simplified by Open Chiari". On phones the filters open, and a filter with no matches says "No studies match these filters."
- **Accessibility scan:** axe (WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rules) found **0 violations on all 17 pages, at both desktop and iPhone size**. Touch targets on phones are 44 px or more, apart from links inside sentences and two 42 px footer links ("Impact", "Privacy"), which are within WCAG 2.2's 24 px minimum.
- **Keyboard (first 20 or more Tab stops on Home and the Explorer):** a skip link comes first. The order follows the page (header, then hero buttons, then tool list, then footer; in the Explorer: header, condition, layers, cut-away, model, 3D view, labels). Focus is clearly visible everywhere except item 17.
