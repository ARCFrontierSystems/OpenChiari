## 1. Square social post: what Chiari is (replaces "1 picture of Chiari, explained.")

> **What is Chiari malformation?** *(Home)*
>
> The lowest part of the back of the brain extends below the opening at the base of the skull. *(School sheet, approved Nov 5, without "In Chiari I,")*
>
> Most people are born with the skull differences that lead to it. Some never have symptoms; others first notice them as teens or adults. *(Home, "Who has it")*
>
> The classic sign is a headache at the back of the head after coughing, sneezing or straining. *(Home, "What it can feel like")*
>
> **Chiari symptoms often have few outward signs. Ask how you can help.** *(Kit quote card)*
>
> See it in 3D at openchiari.org · #ChiariAwareness *(new)*

The Chiari I figure, the approved caption, "Not medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your own care." and the model credit stay as before. The post has no month in it, so it works in September or any other time.

## 3. Make your own (new section on the Awareness kit page)

> **Make your own**
> Choose a design and message, add your own words, a logo or photo, and download it. Everything happens on your device: nothing you add is sent or saved.

- **Design:** Square post · Story · Poster · Sticker sheet
- **Message:** chosen from a list, with the note "Medical facts come only from this list, so every graphic stays accurate." The list:
  - What is Chiari malformation?
  - Chiari symptoms often have few outward signs. Ask how you can help.
  - I have Chiari malformation.
  - Chiari looks different for everyone, and many people with Type I have no symptoms at all.
  - Most people are born with the skull differences that lead to it. Some never have symptoms; others first notice them as teens or adults.
  - The classic sign is a headache at the back of the head after coughing, sneezing or straining.

  All of these are already approved.
- **Your own line (optional),** for example "Walking for my daughter Emma". The note says: "Your own words appear in a separate box, so it is clear they are yours."
- **Name, group or event (optional)** · **Date and place (optional)**
- **Logo or photo (optional),** with the note "It stays on your device."
- **Colors:** Purple · Light · Dark · **Paper size** (poster and stickers): Letter · A4
- **Sticker sheet:** "Your sticker words", with the note "Up to 3 short lines. Shown on all 12 stickers." Custom stickers carry the visitor's words only, not the Open Chiari name.
- **Buttons:** Download image (square post, story) · Print or save as PDF (poster, stickers)

Fixed on every graphic, and not removable: "openchiari.org" and "Not medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your own care." Spanish graphics also carry the translation notice. The poster shows the figure, so it also carries the Chiari I caption and the model credit.

**"For organizations" box (changed).** It used to offer custom kits by email. Now:

The button changes from "Contact Open Chiari" to "Make your own".
