# Make your own, reworked: wording for Matthew's approval (November 5, 2026)

**Status: approved by Matthew on Nov 5 ("Ok on the make your own rework"), including the added headache sentence; recorded in COPY.md. Was: draft. Independent check: passed (`docs/audits/2026-11-05-make-your-own-audit.md`); Spanish labels: back-translation check 5.** Matthew asked on Nov 5 to rework "Make your own" and to implement all of the audit's recommendations. He added: "I don't like the other wording options, out of context, they just read as a continuation of the first card's text option."

He's right. The message list was cut from the ready-made square post, where the lines follow "What is Chiari malformation?" in order. On their own, "Most people are born with the skull differences that lead to **it**" and "**The** classic sign is…" don't say what "it" is.

**The fix:** every message now names Chiari itself, so each one stands alone (line 3 already said "Chiari symptoms" and is unchanged). The facts and their sources are unchanged: each line is an approved sentence with only its subject made explicit. "What is Chiari malformation?" is no longer offered as a bare question. It always comes with its approved answer underneath.

## 5. Independent check (Nov 5)

Every English line, the label and every Spanish line passed (`docs/audits/2026-11-05-make-your-own-audit.md`, sources opened: NINDS web page and PDF, Cleveland Clinic, Baisden 2012, saved PMC12888059; StatPearls and Mayo Clinic blocked the check and were not used).

**One recommendation, now in line 6.** On the ready-made post the headache line came right after "Some never have symptoms". Shown alone, it could suggest everyone with Chiari gets this headache, or that a cough headache means Chiari. The check suggested adding the already-approved sentence "Not everyone with Chiari gets these headaches." (EVIDENCE row 20). It is in the draft above; say if you'd rather leave it out.

**Spanish labels** (back-translation check 5, `docs/audits/2026-11-05-spanish-backtranslation-5.md`): no meaning errors. Small fixes applied: "cada gráfico" instead of "cada imagen" (so it isn't confused with the picture of Chiari), "una de nuestras frases", "Se agrega la imagen… del modelo 3D", "Caminamos por mi hija Emma", and the kit's poster card is now "Póster y folleto para eventos" (was "Cartel…"), to match "Póster" in the tool.

**For the medical translator** (already in the approved Spanish, not new): "síntoma clásico" vs English "classic sign", and "hacer un esfuerzo" vs "straining" ("hacer fuerza" or "pujar" may be closer).
