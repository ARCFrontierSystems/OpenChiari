# Research studies: plain summaries for Matthew's approval (November 5, 2026)

**Status: draft.** None of this is on the site. Until approved, each card shows the first two sentences of the official summary, marked "From the official listing".

Each summary was written from the full ClinicalTrials.gov listing (API v2, opened November 5, 2026). Saved copies of the listings: `docs/audits/sources/2026-11-05-studies/`. An independent check against those listings is in `docs/audits/2026-11-05-study-summaries-audit.md`.

## Decisions made (Matthew, November 5, 2026)

- **Left out:** NCT06375759. Its exclusion criteria list "Chiari-malformation"; it is for a syrinx after a spinal injury or infection.
- **The 2-year rule:** a study whose status hasn't been confirmed in the last 2 years is hidden automatically.
  - This hides NCT06308367 (betaine) and NCT06268093 (thalidomide). Both were last confirmed in February 2024, and their start dates are still "estimated".
  - (Corrected after the independent check.) ClinicalTrials.gov also uses 2 years without a check as a limit: it marks a status "unknown" once a study has gone 2 years unchecked *and* its planned end date has passed. These two studies end in 2027 and 2028, which is why they still read "Recruiting".
- The drafts for those three studies are no longer needed.

## Drafts (revised after the independent check)

| Study | Plain summary (what it's trying to learn) | What you'd do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NCT06502431, Analysis of Disturbances in Neurofluid Dynamics in Type I Chiari Malformations (Amiens, France) | Researchers want to understand how spinal fluid and blood move in the head and neck in Chiari I. They use a special MRI that measures flow, comparing adults with and without symptoms to people without Chiari. | Extra flow scans added to an MRI, on one day. For adults 18 to 50 with no past brain or spine surgery and no other nerve or heart conditions in their history (ask the study team what counts). Not for people who can't have an MRI, or who are pregnant or breastfeeding. People with Chiari are invited after a neurosurgery visit at the hospital in Amiens, and the team re-checks the scans to confirm Chiari I. |
| NCT06724029, Neurosurgical Outcome Network (hospitals in Italy) | Researchers want to learn what predicts how people do after brain and spine surgery, across many conditions including Chiari I. The study doesn't change anyone's treatment; it only collects health information before surgery and afterwards. | Your health information is collected before surgery and during follow-up afterwards; how often and for how long depends on the condition, and the listing doesn't say. For adults having neurosurgery at one of the hospitals in Italy that are still taking new people (the official listing shows which). |

What changed after the check (`docs/audits/2026-11-05-study-summaries-audit.md`):
- **Amiens:** added the MRI, pregnancy and breastfeeding limits, how people with Chiari are invited, and the scan re-check. "Neurological" became "other nerve conditions (ask the study team what counts)".
- **Italy:**
  - removed "memory and thinking, and mood", which the listing limits to certain other conditions;
  - removed "for up to 5 years", which is how long the whole study runs, not how long each person is followed;
  - added that it doesn't change treatment, and that only some of its hospitals are still taking new people.

**One thing to know:** the Amiens listing marks its main data collection as finished in April 2025 but still says "Recruiting". It may not really be taking new people. Its status was confirmed in July 2026, so the 2-year rule keeps it. The card links to the official listing, which is the final word.

None of these studies says people can take part from home, so none is marked "Remote".
