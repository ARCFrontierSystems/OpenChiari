# A 15-minute screen reader check (VoiceOver on iPhone)

Phase C of the plan Matthew approved on Nov 5. The site's automated checks pass on every page, but only a real screen reader shows how it feels to use. Anyone with an iPhone can do this, and it works best with a blind or low-vision tester from the community.

## Turn VoiceOver on and off

- **On iPhone:** Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver → on. *Or ask Siri: "Turn on VoiceOver."*
- **Basic gestures:**
  - swipe right moves to the next item;
  - swipe left moves back;
  - double-tap opens or presses the item you're on;
  - two-finger swipe up reads everything from the top.
- **Turn it off afterwards:** "Hey Siri, turn off VoiceOver", or triple-click the side button if you've set up the Accessibility Shortcut.

## What to try, and what "good" sounds like

Write down anything that sounds wrong, is skipped, is read twice, or makes you lose your place.

1. **Home** (openchiari.org)
   - Swipe through the top. You should hear the menu, the title, the two buttons and "Start here" with its four links.
   - The picture should be described ("Midline section of the head and neck…"), not just announced as "image".
2. **Menu**
   - Find "More" and double-tap. You should hear that it opened ("expanded"), and the five pages should follow.
3. **3D Explorer**
   - Can you tell what the model shows without seeing it? Listen for "What you're looking at" and the captions.
   - Change the condition to "Chiari I". Does it say what changed?
4. **Reading your MRI report**
   - Use the rotor (twist two fingers on the screen) and choose "Headings". Swipe down to jump from term to term.
5. **Visit prep**
   - Tap "A first visit". You should hear how many questions were ticked.
   - Tick and untick a question. Does it say "checked" and "unchecked"?
6. **Awareness kit**
   - In "Make your own", choose a message, then go to the preview. It should read out what the graphic says.
   - "Copy image description" should say it copied.
7. **A printable**
   - Open a sticker sheet PDF from the kit. VoiceOver should read the words on the stickers in order.
